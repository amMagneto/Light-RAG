# Product FAQ

## Pricing Plans

The Starter plan costs $29 per month. The Growth plan costs $99 per month. Enterprise customers receive custom pricing negotiated with sales. Choosing annual billing gives you two months free compared to paying monthly.

## Service Level Agreement

Growth and Enterprise plans include a 99.9% uptime SLA. For every 0.1% of downtime below the commitment, customers receive service credits worth 10 percent of their monthly fee. The Starter plan includes no SLA.

## Refund Policy

Purchases can be refunded within a 30-day refund window from the date of charge. After the first 14 days, refunds become prorated based on usage. The billing team handles all chargebacks.

## Plan Limits

Starter supports up to 3 projects with an API rate limit of 100 requests per minute. Growth raises the ceiling to 25 projects and 1000 requests per minute. Enterprise offers unlimited projects with negotiable rate limits.
