# Information Security Policy

## Access Control

Access rights follow the principle of least privilege. All accounts undergo quarterly access reviews, and unused accounts are disabled. The break-glass administrator account has its credentials rotated every 90 days.

## Vendor Requirements

Every vendor must hold ISO-27001 certification before contract signature. SOC 2 Type II reports are preferred but optional. Procurement sends an annual security questionnaire to all active vendors.

## Data Protection

Customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. Records are kept for a retention period of 24 months, after which a purge job deletes them permanently.

## Password Standards

Passwords must contain at least 16 characters. Any exception requires manager approval and a documented risk acceptance. Following current guidance, there is no scheduled password rotation.
