# HR Handbook

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to four days per week, with a minimum of one day per week in the office. Every remote employee receives an equipment stipend of $500 per year for home office gear. Remote staff must maintain at least four hours of daily timezone overlap with their team.

## Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. Sick leave provides an additional 10 days annually. New parents receive parental leave of six months at full pay.

## Termination and Offboarding

Resignations require a notice period of 60 days. Departing employees receive a severance payment of two months salary for every year served. An exit interview is scheduled within 7 days of the last working day.

## Compensation Review

Salary bands are reviewed annually in April. The annual bonus is paid each March based on company performance.
